# Two kinds of crawler are named below and BOTH are allowed on purpose.
#
#   Retrieval / answer bots  (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot,
#   Perplexity-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, DuckAssistBot, Applebot)
#   are what make this page eligible to be CITED inside a ChatGPT or Perplexity
#   answer. Blocking these is the single most common way a site disappears from
#   AI search without anyone noticing.
#
#   Training bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended,
#   meta-externalagent, Amazonbot, Bytespider, cohere-ai, Timpibot) feed model
#   training sets. For a new agency with no archive to protect, being known to
#   a model before anyone searches is an asset, not a cost. If the client ever
#   wants out of training while staying citable, flip ONLY the training group
#   to Disallow and leave the retrieval group alone. That distinction is the
#   whole point of listing them separately.
#
# A named group REPLACES the wildcard group for that crawler, so every group
# below is written out in full rather than inheriting from `User-agent: *`.
